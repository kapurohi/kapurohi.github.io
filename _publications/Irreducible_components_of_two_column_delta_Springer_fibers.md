The Δ-Springer fibers Y_{n,λ,s}, introduced by Levinson, Woo, and the second author, generalize Springer fibers for GL_n(ℂ) and give a geometric interpretation of the of the Delta Conjecture from algebraic combinatorics (at t=0). We prove that all irreducible components of the Δ-Springer fiber Y_{n,n−1}=Y_{n,(1^{n−1}),n−1} are smooth. In fact, we prove that any intersection of irreducible components of Y_{n,n−1} is a smooth Hessenberg variety which has the structure of an iterated Grassmannian fiber bundle. We then give a presentation of the singular cohomology ring of each irreducible component of Y_{n,n−1} and a combinatorial formula for the Poincaré polynomial of an arbitrary union of intersections of irreducible components in terms of arm and leg statistics on Dyck paths.
